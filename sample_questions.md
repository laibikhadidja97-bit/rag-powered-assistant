**Q: What is the difference between RAG-Sequence and RAG-Token?**

> The difference between RAG-Sequence and RAG-Token lies in their approach to generating responses:
> 
> 1. **RAG-Sequence**:
>    - Uses a sequence-to-sequence model (Seq2Seq).
>    - Processes the entire passage sequentially.
>    - Produces a continuous response by combining the outputs of individual tokens.
>    - Requires longer passages to perform well due to its sequential nature.
> 
> 2. **RAG-Token**:
>    - Utilizes a token-level attention mechanism.
>    - Focuses on answering individual tokens independently.
>    - Generates a probability distribution over possible tokens for each position in the output sequence.
>    - Allows for more efficient handling of short passages because it processes each token separately.
> 
> In summary, while both methods aim to improve upon traditional sequence-to-sequence models, RAG-Token leverages a token-level attention mechanism to produce more accurate and diverse responses compared to RAG-Sequence, particularly useful for generating short answers.
